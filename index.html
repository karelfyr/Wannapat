The fine print

Lesson policy

Flexible when life happens — with clear limits, so every lesson time is respected.

24 hr

Cancelling & rescheduling

Cancel at least 24 hours ahead and we’ll find a makeup lesson. Later cancellations and no-shows count as used — real emergencies are handled case by case.

12 / 24 wk

Package validity

8 lessons are valid for 12 weeks, 20 lessons for 24 weeks. Each package can be extended once on request — +2 weeks or +4 weeks — for illness or travel.

Always

If Chan cancels

You always get a makeup lesson, and it can be scheduled on any date — even after your package ends.

Separate

Online & onsite

Online and onsite are separate packages. You can hold one of each and use whichever you need.

Upfront

Payment & refunds

Packages are paid before the first lesson; single lessons at booking. Unused lessons expire with the package and are non-refundable.

FAQ

Questions I often get.

Can’t find yours? .

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — your first lesson is free on YouTube. Warm up with Chan anytime, then book a 1-on-1 when you’re ready. Watch the free lesson.

How long is a lesson?

Every lesson is 60 minutes, one-on-one with Chan, in Thai or English.

I’ve never sung before. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Many students start from scratch — lessons are built around where you are now and where you want to go.

Which language are lessons in?

Thai or English — whichever you’re most comfortable with.

Which styles do you teach?

Musical theatre and stage performance are Chan’s specialities. She also teaches pop, jazz, funk, rock and Thai pop.

Do you teach kids?

Yes — online from age 7, and onsite in Bangkok at any age.

What do I need for online lessons?

A laptop is ideal, but an iPad or phone also works. Before your first online lesson:

  • Download the Zoom app (not the browser version) so you can adjust the sound
  • Switch Zoom to musician-quality sound — Chan’s short video shows you how
  • Find a private space where you can sing at full voice without distractions
  • Bonus: room to stand and move around a little

Zoom sound setup video

Where are onsite lessons?

At Chan’s studio in Bangkok, with limited availability. The exact location is shared after you book.

I’m in Thailand. How do prices and payment work?

Local rates are available, paid by Thai bank transfer. Message Chan on LINE to get them.

What if I miss a lesson or my package runs out?

Cancel 24 hours ahead and you’ll get a makeup lesson. Each package can be extended once (+2 or +4 weeks). Unused lessons expire with the package and aren’t refundable — see the lesson policy above.

Do you offer group lessons or audition and recital prep?

Possibly — these are arranged one by one. with what you have in mind.

Can I talk to Chan before booking?

Of course — text her on LINE (iamlittlemoon) with any questions before you book.
